Chickostan is a habit counter for one person. You name habits, pin one of them, and add a tick only to the habit that is pinned.

• Name habits and see today’s count on Today.
• Pin one habit, then tick. A tick adds one and releases the pin.
• A tick on any other habit is kept as a miss and does not add.
• A tick before a pin is refused and does not change a count.
• Peel drops the latest tick or miss from today.
• Set an optional daily cap. The count can still go over it.
• Open Log for a seven-day chart and lists of ticks, misses, refused ticks, removed ticks, and closed days.
• Rename or delete a habit, reset today, or erase all data.
• Show the introduction again from Settings.
• Contact and support opens the support page.

Counts stay on this device.
