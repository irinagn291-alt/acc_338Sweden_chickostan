The app does not request any permissions. There is no camera, photo, microphone, location, or tracking usage description in the build settings. There is no login, no in-app purchase, no posted user-generated content, no account deletion flow. Habit names are typed on the device and are not posted. Erase all data removes the local habits and counts. It is not an account deletion. Chickostan is a personal habit counter. It is for one person who wants to pin a single habit and add a tick only to that habit. A tick elsewhere is stored as a miss. No credentials or demo account are needed. On a device, a cold launch shows Today, then a three-page introduction: Page 1 of 3, Count one habit at a time; Page 2 of 3, Tick only what you pinned; Page 3 of 3, Peel a wrong tap. Next moves forward, Back returns one page, and Skip or Start leaves the introduction. With no habits yet, the app adds one named Reps and opens Today. Pin, Tick, and Peel are on Today. Log opens the seven-day charts. Settings opens Daily caps, Lock, Habit list, Ticks, Misses, Refused ticks, Removed ticks, Reset today, Erase all data, and Show introduction. In the Simulator, a first launch may already be past the introduction, with Pushups, Espresso, Pages, and Water on the board and Pushups pinned. Habits and counts stay on this device. Settings includes Contact and support, which opens the support page. Dates, chart labels, and times follow the device calendar and locale. There is no in-app region picker and no region-specific feature set. No licence or regulated-industry paperwork applies. This is a personal habit log, not a gambling, health, or financial product. The app is a personal log only and gives no recommendations. It does not scan barcodes or QR codes.
